PLAIZIEROS – PUT IT ON YOUR TV
==============================

You need: this folder on a laptop, the TV, and both on the same Wi-Fi.
No Android Studio. The installer downloads Google's small "adb" tool (7 MB) by itself.

1. On the TV (only once)
   - Settings > System > About > click "Android TV OS build" 7 times
   - Settings > System > Developer options > turn on "USB debugging"
   - Settings > Network & Internet > your Wi-Fi > write down the IP address

2. On the laptop
   - Double-click Install-PlaizierOS.bat
     (if Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info > Run anyway)

3. Type the TV's IP address, choose OK on the TV, and answer the questions.
   The installer checks that PlaizierOS starts before it makes PlaizierOS your home screen.

Update later: run the installer again with the new PlaizierOS.apk. Your theme and favourites stay.
Undo: Back-to-Google-TV.bat
Tried "Draw at 1920x1080" and it feels slower? Back-to-720p.bat undoes just that.
Something not working? The installer saves plaizieros-report.txt (or run PlaizierOS-report.bat): send it to Claude.

SPOTIFY (a song from your playlist "Oasis" on the home screen) – once
  1. On github.com/WB2810-C/lumen: Settings > Pages > Source "Deploy from a branch",
     branch "main", folder "/docs" > Save. (This is the page Spotify sends you back to.)
  2. On developer.spotify.com/dashboard (log in with your Spotify Premium account): Create app.
     Name: PlaizierOS. Redirect URI: https://wb2810-c.github.io/lumen/spotify.html
     Tick "Web API" > Save. Open the app and copy the Client ID.
  3. On your phone, open the PlaizierOS remote (More > Phone remote, scan the QR code),
     paste the Client ID under Spotify, tap Log in and agree.
  Spotify has to be installed on the TV. The playlist must be yours (made by you) and called "Oasis".

APPLE CALENDAR – once
  1. On account.apple.com: Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords > + > name it PlaizierOS.
  2. In the PlaizierOS remote on your phone, under Apple Calendar: your Apple ID and that password.
